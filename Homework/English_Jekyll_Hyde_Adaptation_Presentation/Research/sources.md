## Other facts checked against the texts

- Hyde tramples "a girl of maybe eight or ten" ("Story of the Door"); his house is in Soho ("Search for Mr. Hyde"); he beats Sir Danvers Carew to death ("The Carew Murder Case").
- Jekyll is "a large, well-made, smooth-faced man of fifty" ("Dr. Jekyll Was Quite at Ease"). He changes without the potion in Regent's Park, and his first supply of salt was "impure" (Full Statement).
- Utterson and Poole find Hyde dead, a "self-destroyer" ("The Last Night").
- Griffin arrives at the Coach and Horses in Iping "early in February" in snow (Ch. I). The villagers are "Sussex villagers" (Ch. III). He robbed his father, who "shot himself" (Ch. XIX).
- Griffin's food shows "until it is assimilated" (Ch. XXV). He kills Wicksteed with an iron rod (Ch. XXVI). He is beaten by a crowd in Port Burdock and his body becomes visible (Ch. XXVIII). Marvel secretly keeps his three notebooks (Epilogue).
