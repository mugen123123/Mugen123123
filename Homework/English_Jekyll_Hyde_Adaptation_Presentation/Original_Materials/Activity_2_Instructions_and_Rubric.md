Adaptations of Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde
Required Materials
Adaptation of Jekyll and Hyde (film, tv show, or publication)
Presentation software
Since Stevenson published The Strange Case of Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde in 1886, there has been no end to the number of adaptations of the story in literature, film, and TV. In this activity, you’ll read or watch an adaptation of Jekyll and Hyde in order to compare and contrast the original version with the adaptation.
Step 1: Find an Adaptation
Below are several options for adaptations of Stevenson’s novella (not to mention several film adaptations by the same name). Choose one from the list or another from your own search to watch or read for this activity.
H.G. Wells’s The Invisible Man (book)
The Nutty Professor (1963 film and 1996 film)
The Incredible Hulk (comic book, TV, film)
Mary Reilly (film)
Hyde, Jekyll, Me (S. Korean TV)
Do No Harm (TV)
Step 2: Create a Slideshow Presentation
Your slideshow should show the similarities and differences between Stevenson’s novella and the adaptation you chose. Include a comparison and contrast for the following elements:
Setting
Characters
Plot
Conflict
Theme(s)
Step 3: Add Components to the Presentation
In your presentation, be sure to include
A title slide
Headings on each slide
Images
A works cited slide
